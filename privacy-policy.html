Countryside Escape Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Countryside Escape Tycoon ("the game") is a campsite management game for iPhone and iPad, made by Wayne Byrne, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("I", "me").

The short version

The game does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device and is never sent to me or anyone else.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your saved game and settings. The game saves your campsites, money, objectives, awards and settings (such as music volume and zoom) automatically, on your device only. This is needed for the game to work. It never leaves your device and I cannot see it.
  • Nothing else. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No adverts and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, from me or from anyone else.
  • No internet connection is needed or used while you play. The game works fully offline.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Apple

You download the game from Apple's App Store. Apple handles the download (and any payment) under its own privacy policy, which you can read at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. If you have chosen to share data with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give me general, anonymous statistics (for example, crash reports or how many people downloaded the game). These do not identify you, and I do not add anything to them.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you are in control:

  • In the game: Menu → Restart game deletes your saved progress for every site.
  • On your device: deleting the app removes all of its data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have rights over personal data held about you, such as the right to access it or have it deleted. Because the game does not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you contact me by email, I will only use your email address and message to reply to you, and I will delete them once your question is dealt with.

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and change the date above before that version of the game is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: countrysideescapetycoon@outlook.com
